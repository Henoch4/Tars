## Model Architecture

### Head A — Magnitude (Quantile Regression)
- Two LightGBM quantile regressors on `y_sum_7d_bps`
- alpha=0.5: central estimate (mean regression, conservative-ish)
- alpha=0.2: lower quantile (conservative entry gate)
- Objective: pinball loss; no grid, fixed hyperparameters (PBO discipline)

### Head B — Duration (Flip Probability)
- LightGBM binary classifier on `y_flip` (funding goes negative within 7d)
- spell-age (bars since last funding sign change) included as covariate
- Survival curve S(k) = Π(1−h_t) makes holding horizon endogenous
- No hand-gridding of 72h vs 7d; the model reads off the optimal horizon

### Decision Layer (Arithmetic, Not Learned)
- Enter when lower-bound E[PnL] > cost hurdle
- E[PnL] = τ=0.2 quantile × survival-weighted horizon − cost_hurdle
- cost_huddle = 16 bps (entry gate), 35 bps (round-trip round-charged)
- Exit on hazard spike (flip probability threshold)

### Calibration (Headline Metric, Ahead of RMSE)
- Isotonic regression on validation slice
- Cross-sectional decile plot: predicted vs realized persistence by decile
- This plot would have caught v2's 'confidence does not rank trades' failure live
- Per-regime OOS breakdown: ensure one funding epoch isn't carrying the model

### Policy Backtest (Kill Criterion)
- NET carry collected by model-driven open/close vs incumbent fixed 0.001 threshold
- NOT RMSE. The model must clear the gate on net carry after costs.
- Kill criterion: beat zero-RMSE + per-symbol climatology OOS + calibrate across deciles
- If cleared: shadow mode (log 'ml_shadow_prediction' entries via src/audit_trail.py),
  then enabled_signals rollout path in ML_ROADMAP Phase 5
- If any fail: honest pivot call, thesis closed (same branch that ended directional ML)
